01 / The signal

Fewer residential units
are being started.

The recorded number of residential units entering construction has fallen sharply in recent years. That is the signal. Now we need to understand the pipeline behind it.

03 / The caveat

But how much of
the pipeline is visible?

A delay can only be measured when all three dates are recorded. This is the share of started-residential records with a submission date, an approval date and a construction-start date.

05 / The scale

So, everything is all right?
There is no problem with approval dates.

Not so fast. Most applications in this dataset are small projects—often a single home or a two-home garden extension. The shape of the application data matters when we read the delays.
