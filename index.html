03 / The caveat

But how much of
the pipeline is visible?

A delay can only be measured when all three dates are recorded. This is the share of started-residential records with a submission date, an approval date and a construction-start date.

Units is the default: units in records with all three dates divided by all started residential units. Applications shows the original record-weighted measure: records with all three dates divided by all started applications.

One source.
Clear calculations.

The main snapshot includes only records where status.raw is Completed or Commenced, proposed residential units are greater than zero, and actual_commencement_date falls between 2000 and 2025. The submission chart uses a separate residential-only query for all statuses with valid_date in 2000–2025. The browser calculates all measures from the returned fields.
